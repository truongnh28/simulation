Formulation · Datasets · Regression

1. Problem Formulation

Ký hiệu: vector $\mathbf{x}$ (thường), ma trận $\mathbf{X}$ (in hoa). Bài toán ML: $\theta^*=\arg\min_\theta \mathcal{L}(f_\theta(\mathbf{X}),\mathbf{Y})$
TaskOutputActivationLoss
Binary clf1 logitSigmoidBCE
Multi-classK logitsSoftmaxCE
Regression1 scalarNoneMSE/MAE
Multi-labelK logitsSigmoid×KBCE×K

2. Datasets

Image tensor: $(B,C,H,W)$; Sequence: $(B,T,D)$. Tỷ lệ split: 70/15/15 hoặc 80/10/10. Stratified split giữ tỷ lệ class. Augmentation chỉ trên train set. Normalize: $\hat{x}=\frac{x-\mu_\text{train}}{\sigma_\text{train}}$ (dùng train stats cho val/test).
⚠ Data leakage: normalize trước split → dùng test info → kết quả ảo. Shuffle time-series → future leak.

3. Linear Regression

$\hat{\mathbf{Y}}=\mathbf{X}\mathbf{W}+\mathbf{b}$. Augment: $\tilde{\mathbf{X}}=[\mathbf{X}|\mathbf{1}]$, $\tilde{\mathbf{W}}=[\mathbf{W};\mathbf{b}]$.
OLS (closed-form): $\tilde{\mathbf{W}}^*=(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}^\top\tilde{\mathbf{X}})^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf{X}}^\top\mathbf{Y}$, độ phức tạp $O(D^3)$.
Ridge: $\tilde{\mathbf{W}}^*=(\tilde{\mathbf{X}}^\top\tilde{\mathbf{X}}+\lambda\mathbf{I})^{-1}\tilde{\mathbf{X}}^\top\mathbf{Y}$ (luôn khả nghịch).

4. Logistic Regression

Binary: $\sigma(z)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-z}}$. Multi-class: $\hat{p}_k=\frac{e^{z_k}}{\sum_j e^{z_j}}$.
Gradient $\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial z = \hat{p}-y$ (đẹp, nên dùng logits).
⚠ PyTorch: KHÔNG dùng Sigmoid trước BCEWithLogitsLoss; KHÔNG dùng Softmax trước CrossEntropyLoss. Các hàm này nhận raw logits.

5. Loss Functions

$\text{MSE}=\frac{1}{n}\sum(y-\hat{y})^2$; $\text{MAE}=\frac{1}{n}\sum|y-\hat{y}|$; Huber=MAE nếu $|e|>\delta$ else $\frac{e^2}{2\delta}$.
$\text{BCE}=-[y\log\hat{p}+(1-y)\log(1-\hat{p})]$
$\text{CE}=-\log\hat{p}_\text{correct}$. Init loss $\approx\log K$ (K classes).
Focal: $-(1-\hat{p}_t)^\gamma\log\hat{p}_t$ (down-weight easy negatives, $\gamma=2$).
Dice Loss: $1-\frac{2\sum p_i g_i}{\sum p_i+\sum g_i}$ (class imbalance).

6. Metrics

$\text{Precision}=\frac{TP}{TP+FP}$; $\text{Recall}=\frac{TP}{TP+FN}$; $F_1=\frac{2PR}{P+R}$
$\text{Balanced Acc}=\frac{\text{Recall}+\text{Specificity}}{2}$; Specificity$=\frac{TN}{TN+FP}$
$\text{Macro-}F_1=\frac{1}{K}\sum F_{1,k}$ (treat classes equally);
$\text{Micro-}F_1=\frac{2\sum TP_k}{2\sum TP_k+\sum FP_k+\sum FN_k}$ (weighted by freq).
Regression: $R^2=1-\frac{\sum(y-\hat{y})^2}{\sum(y-\bar{y})^2}$; $\text{RMSE}=\sqrt{\text{MSE}}$.
✓ Dùng Macro-F1 khi class imbalanced. Micro-F1 ≈ Accuracy nếu balanced.

7. Model Capacity

$\mathbb{E}[(\hat{y}-y)^2]=\text{Bias}^2+\text{Variance}+\sigma^2_\epsilon$
Underfitting: high bias (model quá đơn giản). Overfitting: high variance (model quá phức tạp).
Tăng capacity: thêm neuron/layer, feature transform $\Phi(\mathbf{x})=[1,x,x^2,\ldots]$.
⚠ Regularization: L2 $+\lambda\|\mathbf{W}\|_2^2$, L1 $+\lambda\|\mathbf{W}\|_1$ (sparse), Dropout, Early stopping.

MLP · Training · Optimizers

8. MLP

Forward: $\mathbf{h}^{(l)}=\varphi(\mathbf{W}^{(l)}\mathbf{h}^{(l-1)}+\mathbf{b}^{(l)})$
Params FC layer $M\to N$: $M\cdot N+N=N(M+1)$
MLP stack $[D_0,D_1,D_2,D_3]$: params$=D_1(D_0+1)+D_2(D_1+1)+D_3(D_2+1)$

BatchNorm vs LayerNorm

BN: $\hat{x}=\frac{x-\mu_B}{\sqrt{\sigma_B^2+\epsilon}}$; $y=\gamma\hat{x}+\beta$. Norm qua batch dim (B). Học $\gamma,\beta$. Train/eval khác nhau (running stats).
LN: Norm qua feature dim. Dùng cho Transformer, sequence. Không phụ thuộc batch size.

9. Training ANNs

SGD + Momentum

$\mathbf{v}_t=\mu\mathbf{v}_{t-1}+\mathbf{g}_t$; $\theta_t=\theta_{t-1}-\eta\mathbf{v}_t$ ($\mu=0.9$)
Momentum giúp vượt local minima nông, giảm oscillation.

Adam

$\mathbf{m}_t=\beta_1\mathbf{m}_{t-1}+(1-\beta_1)\mathbf{g}_t$ (1st moment)
$\mathbf{v}_t=\beta_2\mathbf{v}_{t-1}+(1-\beta_2)\mathbf{g}_t^2$ (2nd moment)
Bias correction: $\hat{\mathbf{m}}_t=\frac{\mathbf{m}_t}{1-\beta_1^t}$; $\hat{\mathbf{v}}_t=\frac{\mathbf{v}_t}{1-\beta_2^t}$
Update: $\theta_t=\theta_{t-1}-\eta\frac{\hat{\mathbf{m}}_t}{\sqrt{\hat{\mathbf{v}}_t}+\epsilon}$
Defaults: $\beta_1=0.9$, $\beta_2=0.999$, $\epsilon=10^{-8}$

AdamW

Decoupled weight decay: $\theta_t=(1-\eta\lambda)\theta_{t-1}-\eta\frac{\hat{\mathbf{m}}_t}{\sqrt{\hat{\mathbf{v}}_t}+\epsilon}$
Adam: WD qua L2 penalty (vào gradient) → bị adaptive scaling làm yếu.
AdamW: WD tách riêng → đúng nghĩa regularization.

LR Schedulers

Cosine annealing: $\eta_t=\frac{\eta_0}{2}\left(1+\cos\frac{t\pi}{T}\right)$
Warmup: tăng tuyến tính $\eta$ trong $T_w$ bước đầu → ổn định early training.
Step decay: $\eta\leftarrow\eta\cdot\gamma$ mỗi $k$ epoch.

Regularization Tricks

Dropout: zero out mỗi neuron với xác suất $p$ khi train; scale $\times\frac{1}{1-p}$ (inverted). Tắt khi inference.
Grad clipping: $\mathbf{g}\leftarrow\mathbf{g}\cdot\frac{\tau}{\max(\|\mathbf{g}\|,\tau)}$ (tránh exploding grad).
Early stopping: dừng khi val loss không giảm sau patience epochs.
✓ Học tốt: train loss↓ val loss↓. Overfit: train↓ val↑. Underfit: cả 2 cao.

Backprop Chain Rule

$\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\mathbf{W}^{(l)}}=\frac{\partial\mathcal{L}}{\partial\mathbf{h}^{(l)}}\cdot\frac{\partial\mathbf{h}^{(l)}}{\partial\mathbf{z}^{(l)}}\cdot\frac{\partial\mathbf{z}^{(l)}}{\partial\mathbf{W}^{(l)}}$
Vanishing: sigmoid/tanh sâu → dùng ReLU + skip connections. Exploding: grad clipping.

CNN — Công Thức & Bài Tập

10. CNN Core

Output size: $o=\left\lfloor\frac{i+2p-k}{s}\right\rfloor+1$
$i$=input, $p$=padding, $k$=kernel, $s$=stride
Conv params: $K\times(k^2\times D_{in}+1)$ ($K$=filters, bias per filter)
No-bias: $K\times k^2\times D_{in}$
Depthwise Sep Conv params: $k^2\times D_{in}+D_{in}\times D_{out}$ (vs $k^2 D_{in} D_{out}$)
MaxPool 2×2 s=2: $H,W\to H/2,W/2$ (no learnable params)

Cross-Corr vs Convolution

XCorr (PyTorch Conv2d, không flip W):
$Y(u,v)=\sum_{i,j}X(u+i,v+j)\cdot W(i,j)$
Conv (toán học, flip W 180°):
$Y(u,v)=\sum_{i,j}X(u-i,v-j)\cdot W(i,j)$
Conv$(W)=$XCorr$(\text{Rot}_{180}(W))$

Sample Problem: Tính Output Size

Input: (1,3,32,32), Conv(16,3,p=1,s=1): o = ⌊(32+2×1-3)/1⌋+1 = 32 → (1,16,32,32) Params = 16×(3²×3+1) = 16×28 = 448 MaxPool(2,s=2): 32→16 → (1,16,16,16) Conv(32,3,p=1,s=1): o=16 → (1,32,16,16) Params = 32×(3²×16+1) = 32×145 = 4,640

Receptive Field

Stack 2 conv 3×3 (s=1) = RF 5×5 (như conv 5×5 nhưng ít params hơn: $2×9=18$ vs $25$).
RF tăng theo: $r_l=r_{l-1}+(k_l-1)\prod_{i<l}s_i$

Generative Models

11. VAE — Variational Autoencoder

Latent variable model: $p_\theta(x)=\int p_\theta(x|z)p(z)dz$, prior $p(z)=\mathcal{N}(0,I)$.
Encoder: $q_\phi(z|x)=\mathcal{N}(\mu_\phi(x),\sigma^2_\phi(x)I)$
ELBO: $\mathcal{L}=\underbrace{\mathbb{E}_{q_\phi}[\log p_\theta(x|z)]}_{\text{Reconstruction}}-\underbrace{D_{KL}(q_\phi(z|x)\|p(z))}_{\text{Regularization}}$
KL (Gaussian): $D_{KL}=\frac{1}{2}\sum_j(\mu_j^2+\sigma_j^2-\log\sigma_j^2-1)$
Reparameterize: $\mathbf{z}=\mu+\sigma\odot\epsilon$, $\epsilon\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$ → differentiable sampling
⚠ Posterion collapse: decoder bỏ qua z → KL→0, recon tệ. Fix: KL annealing (tăng dần weight KL).
VAE forward (PyTorch): mu, logvar = encoder(x) std = torch.exp(0.5*logvar) z = mu + std * torch.randn_like(std) x_hat = decoder(z) recon = F.mse_loss(x_hat, x) kl = -0.5*(1+logvar-mu**2-logvar.exp()).sum() loss = recon + beta*kl

12. GAN — Generative Adversarial Network

Minimax game: $\min_G\max_D\,\mathbb{E}_x[\log D(x)]+\mathbb{E}_z[\log(1-D(G(z)))]$
D muốn phân biệt real ($D(x)\to1$) vs fake ($D(G(z))\to0$).
G muốn fool D: thực tế train G với $\max\mathbb{E}_z[\log D(G(z))]$ (non-saturating).
ProblemSymptomFix
Mode collapseG tạo 1 kiểuWasserstein GAN
D quá mạnhG gradient→0label smoothing
Instabilityloss oscillateWGAN-GP
WGAN: $\min_G\max_{D\in\text{1-Lip}}\mathbb{E}[D(x)]-\mathbb{E}[D(G(z))]$; gradient penalty thay clip weights.

13. DDPM — Diffusion Models

Forward (noising): $q(x_t|x_{t-1})=\mathcal{N}(x_t;\sqrt{1-\beta_t}x_{t-1},\beta_t I)$
Rút gọn: $x_t=\sqrt{\bar\alpha_t}\,x_0+\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}\,\epsilon$, $\epsilon\sim\mathcal{N}(0,I)$
$\bar\alpha_t=\prod_{s=1}^t(1-\beta_s)$, $\bar\alpha_t\to0$ khi $t\to T$
Training loss: $\mathcal{L}=\mathbb{E}_{t,x_0,\epsilon}\big[\|\epsilon-\epsilon_\theta(x_t,t)\|^2\big]$
Reverse (sampling): $x_{t-1}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\alpha_t}}\left(x_t-\frac{1-\alpha_t}{\sqrt{1-\bar\alpha_t}}\epsilon_\theta\right)+\sigma_t z$
✓ DDPM tốt hơn GAN về diversity; chậm hơn (nhiều denoising steps). DDIM: deterministic, ít steps.

Transformer · Object Detection

14. Transformer / Attention

Scaled Dot-Product Attention:
$\text{Attention}(Q,K,V)=\text{Softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V$
$Q=XW^Q,\;K=XW^K,\;V=XW^V$ — $W^Q,W^K,W^V\in\mathbb{R}^{d_{model}\times d_k}$
Scale $\sqrt{d_k}$: tránh dot-product quá lớn → Softmax saturate → gradient nhỏ.
Multi-Head: $\text{MH}(Q,K,V)=\text{Concat}(h_1,\ldots,h_H)W^O$
mỗi head $h_i=\text{Attention}(QW_i^Q,KW_i^K,VW_i^V)$

Transformer Block

Encoder: MSA → Add&Norm → FFN → Add&Norm (FFN=2 FC, $d_{ff}=4d_{model}$)
Decoder: Masked MSA → Cross-Attention (K,V từ encoder) → FFN.
Causal mask: upper-tri mask $-\infty$ → Softmax→0 → không attend future tokens.

ViT — Vision Transformer

Image $H\times W$ → patches $P\times P$ → $N=\frac{HW}{P^2}$ tokens, mỗi token $=P^2C$ dim → project to $d_{model}$.
Thêm [CLS] token + positional embedding (learned hoặc sinusoidal).
Classify từ [CLS] output hoặc GAP tất cả patch tokens.
ViT-B/16: H=W=224, P=16 → N=196+1=197 tokens d_model=768, 12 heads, 12 blocks Params ≈ 86M

15. Object Detection

YOLO (v8/v11)

Anchor-free: predict offset từ grid cell center.
Số anchors: $80^2+40^2+20^2=6400+1600+400=8400$ (input 640×640)
DFL — Distribution Focal Loss: $\hat{d}=\sum_k k\cdot\text{Softmax}(s_k)$ (integral regression box)
Neck: FPN (top-down) + PAN (bottom-up) → multi-scale features
Head: Decoupled: cls branch + reg branch (không share)
Assignment: TaskAlignedAssigner — score = $p^\alpha\cdot\text{IoU}^\beta$

IoU & Variants

$\text{IoU}=\frac{|A\cap B|}{|A\cup B|}$; $\text{GIoU}=\text{IoU}-\frac{|C\setminus(A\cup B)|}{|C|}$
$\text{DIoU}=\text{IoU}-\frac{d^2}{c^2}$ ($d$=center dist); $\text{CIoU}=\text{DIoU}-\alpha v$ (aspect ratio)

mAP Calculation

1. Sort dets by confidence ↓. 2. TP nếu IoU≥thresh với GT, else FP.
$\text{AP}_c=\int_0^1 p(r)\,dr$ (AUC of PR curve, 11-point interp hoặc all-point).
$\text{mAP}=\frac{1}{N_c}\sum_c\text{AP}_c$; mAP@[.5:.95] = avg mAP qua IoU 0.5→0.95.

NMS — Non-Maximum Suppression

1. Sort boxes by confidence ↓ 2. Keep box with highest conf → remove boxes IoU > τ (e.g. 0.45) with kept box 3. Repeat until empty Soft-NMS: giảm score thay vì xóa hẳn

DETR — Detection Transformer

$N_q$ object queries → Transformer decoder → $N_q$ predictions.
Hungarian matching: bipartite matching loss để assign pred↔GT (1-to-1).
$\mathcal{L}_\text{match}=\lambda_{cls}\mathcal{L}_{cls}+\lambda_{box}\mathcal{L}_1+\lambda_{iou}\mathcal{L}_{iou}$
Không cần NMS! Nhưng cần nhiều GPU, training lâu hơn YOLO.

UNet Architecture

Encoder (contract): Conv→MaxPool×4, depth↑ channels↑
Bottleneck: deepest feature map
Decoder (expand): UpConv + skip connection concat → Conv×2
Output: 1×1 Conv → K-channel segmentation map
Skip connections: encoder features concat với decoder (preserve spatial detail)
UNet channel flow (example): 3→64→128→256→512→1024 (bottleneck) →512→256→128→64→K (num classes)

Segmentation Metrics

$\text{Dice}=\frac{2TP}{2TP+FP+FN}=\frac{2|A\cap B|}{|A|+|B|}$
$\text{IoU}=\frac{TP}{TP+FP+FN}=\frac{|A\cap B|}{|A\cup B|}$
Quan hệ: $\text{Dice}=\frac{2\text{IoU}}{1+\text{IoU}}$; $\text{IoU}=\frac{\text{Dice}}{2-\text{Dice}}$
$\text{mIoU}=\frac{1}{K}\sum_{k=0}^{K-1}\text{IoU}_k$
$\text{HD95}$: 95th percentile of Hausdorff distances (surface error, medical imaging)
Panoptic Quality: $PQ=\underbrace{SQ}_{\text{seg quality}}\times\underbrace{RQ}_{\text{recog quality}}$
$SQ=\frac{\sum_{(p,g)\in TP}\text{IoU}(p,g)}{|TP|}$; $RQ=\frac{|TP|}{|TP|+\frac{1}{2}|FP|+\frac{1}{2}|FN|}$

Loss Functions for Segmentation

Dice Loss: $1-\frac{2\sum p_i g_i+\epsilon}{\sum p_i+\sum g_i+\epsilon}$ (smooth ε prevent div/0)
Focal Loss: $(1-p_t)^\gamma(-\log p_t)$ (class imbalance)
Combined: Dice + CE (best practice)
⚠ Resize mask → dùng nearest neighbor, KHÔNG dùng bilinear (tạo class values không hợp lệ). Trong PyTorch: mode='nearest'.

Critical Warnings / Exam Traps

⚠ BCEWithLogitsLoss / CrossEntropyLoss: nhận raw logits, KHÔNG thêm Sigmoid/Softmax trước.
⚠ BatchNorm train vs eval: model.eval() → dùng running mean/var, không phải batch stats. Quên eval() → kết quả không ổn định.
⚠ Augmentation chỉ train set: val/test chỉ normalize. Normalize dùng train stats.
⚠ OLS closed-form: O(D³) → không dùng nếu D lớn (features nhiều), dùng gradient descent.
⚠ mAP@.5 vs mAP@[.5:.95]: sau là strict hơn, COCO dùng mAP@[.5:.95].
⚠ YOLO 8400 = $80^2+40^2+20^2$ (với input 640×640, 3 scales).
