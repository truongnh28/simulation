📍 K-Nearest Neighbors — Ch.3
▶ Khoảng cách
\(L_2: d(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{x}') = \sqrt{\sum_d (x_d - x'_d)^2}\)
\(L_1: d(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{x}') = \sum_d |x_d - x'_d|\)
Minkowski: \(\!\left(\sum_d |x_d-x'_d|^p\right)^{1/p}\) [p=2→L₂, p=1→L₁]
▶ KD-Tree vs Ball-Tree
Brute-force: \(\mathcal{O}(N\cdot D)\) mỗi query
KD-Tree: \(\text{dim}^*=\arg\max_d\text{Var}(X_d)\), split median → depth \(\mathcal{O}(\log N)\)
Prune: \(d(q,\text{hyperplane})>r_\text{best}\) → skip subtree
Ball-Tree: Prune nếu \(d(q,c)-r>r_\text{best}\)
⚠️ Curse of Dim: D lớn → KD-Tree thoái hóa \(\mathcal{O}(N\cdot D)\). Ball-Tree tốt hơn khi D vừa.
▶ Siêu tham số K
\(K=1\): Train Err \(=0\) (self = nearest neighbor) → OVERFIT
\(K=N\): Predict majority → Train Err \(=1-\max_c P(c)\) → UNDERFIT
\(K^*\approx\sqrt{N}\): optimal (cross-validation)
KTrain ErrTest ErrHiện tượng
10CaoOverfit
\(\sqrt{N}\)ThấpThấp nhấtOptimal

▶ Quy trình thi
1
Tính \(d(q,x_i)\) cho mọi training point
2
Sort, lấy K nearest neighbors
3
Majority vote → class. Normalize features!
🔮 Naive Bayes — Ch.4
▶ Bayes Theorem
\(P(C|\mathbf{X}) = \dfrac{P(C)\cdot P(\mathbf{X}|C)}{P(\mathbf{X})}\)

Log-Odds (tuyến tính!):
\(\log\dfrac{P(+|\mathbf{X})}{P(-|\mathbf{X})} = \log\dfrac{P(+)}{P(-)} + \sum_k\log\dfrac{P(x_k|+)}{P(x_k|-)}\)
▶ Conditional Independence (CI)
\(P(X_1,\ldots,X_n|C)=\prod_k P(X_k|C)\) [CI]
Params: \(2^n-1 \;\to\; n\) per class (exponential → linear!)
⚠️ CI vi phạm → double-count evidence → posterior overconfident. Vẫn dùng được vì chỉ cần argmax.
▶ Laplace Smoothing
Vấn đề: count\(=0\;\Rightarrow\;P=0\;\Rightarrow\) tích \(=0\)
\(P(x_k|c) = \dfrac{\text{count}(x_k,c)+\alpha}{\text{count}(c)+\alpha|V|}\)
\(\alpha=1\): MAP với Dirichlet\((1,\ldots,1)\) prior
▶ Multinomial NB (Text)
Underflow: \(P(d|c)\approx(0.001)^{200}=10^{-600}\to 0\)
Log-space: \(\text{score}(c)=\log P(c)+\sum_k x_k\log P(w_k|c)\)

🎯 K-Means Clustering
▶ Hàm mục tiêu WCSS
\(\text{WCSS}=\sum_k\sum_{\mathbf{x}\in C_k}\|\mathbf{x}-\boldsymbol{\mu}_k\|^2\)
Non-convex: không gian \(\boldsymbol{\mu}\) liên tục × \(C\) rời rạc
→ Global min là NP-hard → chỉ tìm local min
▶ Lloyd's Algorithm
1
Init: K-Means++ seed
2
Assignment: \(c_i=\arg\min_k\|\mathbf{x}_i-\boldsymbol{\mu}_k\|^2\)
3
Update: \(\boldsymbol{\mu}_k=\frac{1}{|C_k|}\sum_{\mathbf{x}_i\in C_k}\mathbf{x}_i\)
4
Lặp đến khi WCSS không đổi
▶ Chứng minh Hội tụ
① Assignment: \(\text{WCSS}_\text{after}\leq\text{WCSS}_\text{before}\)
② Update: \(\frac{d}{d\boldsymbol{\mu}}\sum\|\mathbf{x}_i-\boldsymbol{\mu}\|^2=0\Rightarrow\boldsymbol{\mu}^*=\text{mean}\)
③ Configs \(\leq K^N\) (hữu hạn) + monotone\(\downarrow\) → PHẢI dừng ✓
⚠️ Chỉ LOCAL minimum, không phải global!
▶ K-Means++ & L1/L2
K-Means++: \(P(\mathbf{x})\propto d(\mathbf{x},\text{nearest})^2\)
\(\mathbb{E}[\text{WCSS}]\leq 8(\ln K+2)\cdot\text{WCSS}^*\)

L2 → Mean: \(\sum(\mathbf{x}_i-\boldsymbol{\mu})=\mathbf{0}\)
L1 → Median: \(\#\{x_i\!<\!\mu\}=\#\{x_i\!>\!\mu\}\)

📈 Logistic Regression
▶ Logit → Sigmoid (suy ra, không tùy tiện)
\(p\in(0,1)\;\to\;\text{Odds}=\dfrac{p}{1-p}\;\to\;\text{Logit}=\log\dfrac{p}{1-p}\in\mathbb{R}\)

Đặt: \(z=\mathbf{w}\cdot\mathbf{x}+b=\log\dfrac{p}{1-p}\)
Đảo: \(p=\sigma(z)=\dfrac{1}{1+e^{-z}}\)
\(\sigma'(z)=\sigma(z)(1-\sigma(z))\leq\dfrac{1}{4}\) (max tại \(z=0\))
▶ CE Loss = MLE + Convexity
\(P(y|\mathbf{x},\mathbf{w})=\hat{y}^y(1-\hat{y})^{1-y}\)
\(\mathcal{L}_{CE}=-y\log\hat{y}-(1-y)\log(1-\hat{y})\)\;→ Min CE = MLE ✓

\(\dfrac{\partial\mathcal{L}_{CE}}{\partial z}=\hat{y}-y\) [\(\sigma'(z)\) triệt tiêu hoàn toàn]
\(\mathbf{H}=\sum_i\sigma_i(1-\sigma_i)\mathbf{x}_i\mathbf{x}_i^\top\succeq\mathbf{0}\) → CONVEX
▶ MSE Vanishing Gradient
\(\dfrac{\partial\mathcal{L}_{MSE}}{\partial z}=(\hat{y}-y)\cdot\hat{y}(1-\hat{y})\)
Khi \(\hat{y}\to 0\) hoặc \(\hat{y}\to 1\): \(\sigma'\to 0\) → gradient \(\to 0\)
\(L\) lớp sigmoid: \(\leq\left(\tfrac{1}{4}\right)^L\) → \(L=10\): \(10^{-6}\approx 0\)
▶ Precision / Recall / F1
\(\text{Precision}=\dfrac{TP}{TP+FP}\quad\text{Recall}=\dfrac{TP}{TP+FN}\)
\(F_1=\dfrac{2PR}{P+R}\) [harmonic mean, phạt mất cân bằng]
\(\theta\)PRUse case
Thấp (0.2)\(\downarrow\)\(\uparrow\)Ung thư (no miss)
Cao (0.8)\(\uparrow\)\(\downarrow\)Spam filter
▶ L2/L1 Regularization
L2: \(\mathcal{L}+\dfrac{\lambda}{2}\|\mathbf{w}\|^2\leftrightarrow\mathcal{N}(0,1/\lambda)\) → \(\mathbf{w}\neq\mathbf{0}\)
L1: \(\mathcal{L}+\lambda\|\mathbf{w}\|_1\leftrightarrow\text{Laplace}(0,1/\lambda)\) → sparse
⚔️ SVM — Margin, Hard/Soft, Dual
▶ Decision Boundary
DBoundaryPhương trình
1DĐiểm\(w_1x_1+b=0\)
2DĐường thẳng\(w_1x_1+w_2x_2+b=0\)
nDHyperplane (n−1)D\(\mathbf{w}\cdot\mathbf{x}+b=0\)
▶ Hard-margin & Margin = \(2/\|\mathbf{w}\|\)
\(H_1:\mathbf{w}\cdot\mathbf{x}+b=+1\quad H_2:\mathbf{w}\cdot\mathbf{x}+b=-1\)
Constraint: \(y_i(\mathbf{w}\cdot\mathbf{x}_i+b)\geq 1\;\forall i\)
\(\text{Margin}=\dfrac{(\mathbf{x}_+-\mathbf{x}_-)\cdot\mathbf{w}}{\|\mathbf{w}\|}=\dfrac{2}{\|\mathbf{w}\|}\)

\(\min\dfrac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{w}\|^2\) s.t. \(y_i(\mathbf{w}\cdot\mathbf{x}_i+b)\geq 1\)
\(\text{dist}(x_0)=\dfrac{|\mathbf{w}\cdot\mathbf{x}_0+b|}{\|\mathbf{w}\|}\)
▶ Dual Formulation
\(W(\boldsymbol{\alpha})=\sum_i\alpha_i-\dfrac{1}{2}\sum_{ij}\alpha_i\alpha_j y_i y_j(\mathbf{x}_i\cdot\mathbf{x}_j)\)
KKT: \(\mathbf{w}=\sum_i\alpha_i y_i\mathbf{x}_i\), \(\sum_i\alpha_i y_i=0\), \(\alpha_i\geq 0\)
\(\alpha_i>0\leftrightarrow\) Support Vector; \(\alpha_i=0\leftrightarrow\) non-SV
▶ Soft-margin — Slack \(\xi_i\)
\(\min\dfrac{1}{2}\|\mathbf{w}\|^2+C\sum_i\xi_i\quad\text{s.t. }y_i(\mathbf{w}\cdot\mathbf{x}_i+b)\geq 1-\xi_i,\;\xi_i\geq 0\)

\(\xi_i=0\): ngoài margin, đúng phía ✓
\(0<\xi_i\leq 1\): trong margin, đúng phía ✓
\(\xi_i>1\): sai phía = misclassified ✗
CMarginKết quả
\(\to 0\)Rất lớnUnderfit
OptimalVừaBest
\(\to\infty\)Cực nhỏHard-margin
💡 \(C\equiv 1/\lambda\): C nhỏ ↔ \(\lambda\) lớn ↔ regularize mạnh ↔ underfit.
⚔️ SVM — Kernel Trick, RBF, Hinge Loss
▶ Kernel Trick
Dual SVM chỉ dùng \(\mathbf{x}_i\cdot\mathbf{x}_j\)
→ Thay bằng \(K(\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{x}_j)=\varphi(\mathbf{x}_i)\cdot\varphi(\mathbf{x}_j)\)
Predict: \(\hat{y}=\text{sign}\!\left(\sum_i\alpha_i y_i K(\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{x})+b\right)\)
KernelCông thứcFeature space
Linear\(\mathbf{x}^\top\mathbf{x}'\)D chiều
Poly deg-d\((\gamma\mathbf{x}^\top\mathbf{x}'+r)^d\)Poly
RBF\(\exp(-\gamma\|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'\|^2)\)\(\infty\) chiều
▶ RBF: Gram Matrix & hành vi \(\gamma\)
Gram matrix: \(K_{ij}=K(\mathbf{x}_i,\mathbf{x}_j)\), luôn PSD (Mercer)
\(\gamma\to 0\): \(K_{ij}\to 1\) → all-ones → Linear → UNDERFIT
\(\gamma\to\infty\): \(K_{ij}=0\,(i\neq j),\,K_{ii}=1\) → Identity \(\mathbf{I}\)
\(\equiv\) 1-NN → Train Err\(=0\), test OVERFIT
💡 \(\gamma\) = "bán kính ảnh hưởng nghịch đảo". RBF \(\equiv\) \(\infty\)-dim feature map.
▶ Hinge Loss vs Cross-Entropy
Hinge: \(\max(0,\,1-yf(\mathbf{x}))\) — piecewise linear
\(m\geq 1\): gradient \(=0\) → FLAT → SPARSE \(\alpha_i\)

CE: \(\log(1+e^{-m})\) — smooth, gradient \(\neq 0\) mọi lúc → DENSE
Tiêu chíHinge (SVM)CE (LR)
Grad điểm dễ\(=0\) (sparse)\(\neq 0\) (all update)
ObjectiveMax marginMLE
Output\(\text{sign}(f)\)\(\sigma(f)\) prob
Outlier sens.Thấp (C)Cao

2

3
Giải: tìm \(\mathbf{w}\), \(b\). Margin \(=2/\|\mathbf{w}\|\)
4
Predict: \(\text{sign}(\mathbf{w}\cdot\mathbf{x}+b)\)

🌳 Decision Trees — Ch.5
▶ Độ đo tạp chất
Entropy: \(H(S)=-\sum_k p_k\log_2 p_k\in[0,\log_2 K]\)
Gini: \(G(S)=1-\sum_k p_k^2\in[0,1-1/K]\)
Error: \(E(S)=1-\max_k p_k\)
Thứ tự: \(H\geq G\geq E\). Pure node: \(H=G=E=0\)
▶ Information Gain & Gain Ratio
\(\text{IG}(S,A)=H(S)-\sum_v\dfrac{|S_v|}{|S|}H(S_v)\)
\(\text{GainRatio}=\dfrac{\text{IG}(S,A)}{\text{SplitInfo}(S,A)}\)
\(\text{SplitInfo}=-\sum_v\dfrac{|S_v|}{|S|}\log\dfrac{|S_v|}{|S|}\)
▶ CART — Binary Splits với Gini
\(t^*=\arg\min_t\!\left[\dfrac{|L|}{|S|}G(L)+\dfrac{|R|}{|S|}G(R)\right]\)
✓ DT multi-class, không cần normalize, xử lý categorical.
⚠️ IG bias many-valued attrs → dùng GainRatio (C4.5) hoặc Gini (CART).

🧠 Neural Networks — Ch.7
▶ Forward Propagation
\(\mathbf{z}^l=\mathbf{W}^l\mathbf{a}^{l-1}+\mathbf{b}^l\quad\) [linear transform]
\(\mathbf{a}^l=f(\mathbf{z}^l)\quad\) [activation]
▶ Backpropagation
\(\boldsymbol{\delta}^L=\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial\mathbf{z}^L\)
\(\boldsymbol{\delta}^l=(\mathbf{W}^{l+1})^\top\boldsymbol{\delta}^{l+1}\odot f'(\mathbf{z}^l)\)
\(\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial\mathbf{W}^l=\boldsymbol{\delta}^l(\mathbf{a}^{l-1})^\top,\quad\partial\mathcal{L}/\partial\mathbf{b}^l=\boldsymbol{\delta}^l\)

▶ Delta Rule & Optimizers
Perceptron: \(\mathbf{w}\leftarrow\mathbf{w}+\eta(y-\hat{y})\mathbf{x}\)

🔗 Hidden Markov Models
▶ Tham số \(\lambda=(\boldsymbol{\pi},A,B)\)
\(\pi_i=P(q_1=i)\quad A_{ij}=P(q_t=j|q_{t-1}=i)\quad B_j(o)=P(o_t=o|q_t=j)\)
▶ Forward & Viterbi
Forward: \(\alpha_t(j)=\!\left[\sum_i\alpha_{t-1}(i)A_{ij}\right]\!B_j(o_t)\) [SUM]
Init: \(\alpha_1(i)=\pi_i B_i(o_1)\), \(P(O|\lambda)=\sum_i\alpha_T(i)\)

Viterbi: \(\delta_t(j)=\!\left[\max_i\delta_{t-1}(i)A_{ij}\right]\!B_j(o_t)\) [MAX]
\(Q^*=\) backtrack từ \(\arg\max_i\delta_T(i)\)
▶ Baum-Welch (EM)
\(\gamma_t(i)=P(q_t=i|O,\lambda)\), \(\xi_t(i,j)=P(q_t=i,q_{t+1}=j|O,\lambda)\)
\(A_{ij}=\dfrac{\sum_t\xi_t(i,j)}{\sum_t\gamma_t(i)}\quad B_i(k)=\dfrac{\sum_{t:o_t=k}\gamma_t(i)}{\sum_t\gamma_t(i)}\)

🚨 Bẫy Thi Thường Gặp — ĐỪNG nhầm!
  • [KNN] \(K=1\): Train Err\(=0\) không phải underfit — self là nearest neighbor
  • [KNN] KD-Tree mất lợi thế khi D lớn (curse of dim)
  • [NB] "Naive" ≠ yếu — SOTA text; CI vi phạm nhưng argmax vẫn đúng
  • [NB] Zero freq → cả tích \(=0\) → Laplace \(\alpha=1\) bắt buộc
  • [K-Means] WCSS không bao giờ tăng trong Lloyd's
  • [K-Means] Hội tụ = local min, KHÔNG phải global (NP-hard)
  • [LR] \(\sigma(z)\) = nghịch đảo của logit, không phải "squashing" tùy tiện
  • [LR] \(\partial\mathcal{L}_{CE}/\partial z=\hat{y}-y\) (KHÔNG có \(\sigma'\)); MSE = \((\hat{y}-y)\sigma'(z)\) (CÓ)
  • [LR] L2→\(\mathbf{w}\neq\mathbf{0}\) (sphere). L1→\(\mathbf{w}=\mathbf{0}\) sparse (diamond)
  • [SVM] C lớn→overfit (\(C=1/\lambda\); \(\lambda\) lớn↔underfit)
  • [SVM] \(\gamma\to\infty\) → Gram\(=\mathbf{I}\) → Train Err\(=0\) (1-NN, overfit)
  • [SVM] Hinge\(=0\) khi \(m\geq 1\), KHÔNG phải \(m\geq 0\)
  • [SVM] Chỉ SVs (\(\alpha_i>0\)) ảnh hưởng \(\mathbf{w}\)
  • [DT] IG bias many-valued → GainRatio (C4.5) hoặc Gini (CART)
  • [NN] Sigmoid \(L\) lớp: gradient \(\leq(0.25)^L\) → dùng ReLU
  • [Ensemble] Bagging\(\downarrow\)Variance, Boosting\(\downarrow\)Bias — KHÔNG đổi!
  • [HMM] Forward=SUM (tất cả paths). Viterbi=MAX (path tốt nhất)
  • [General] Normalize: KNN, SVM, LR, NN cần. DT, RF, NB không cần
